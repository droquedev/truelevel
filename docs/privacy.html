TrueLevel Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2026

TrueLevel (“the app”) is a bubble level for Android published under the application ID com.droque.truelevel. This policy describes what the Play Store release of TrueLevel does with data.

We do not collect personal data

TrueLevel does not collect, share, sell, or transmit personal information. It does not include advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or accounts. The release build does not request internet permission and does not make network requests.

What stays on your device

The app reads the phone’s gravity sensor, or the accelerometer if gravity is unavailable, to show tilt. Those readings are processed on the device and are never sent anywhere.

If you calibrate, the zero-point offsets are stored only in the app’s private local storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Reset in the app also deletes them.

Permissions

TrueLevel does not request runtime permissions. It requires a device with an accelerometer because that is the hardware the level uses.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the GitHub repository that hosts this page. If you publish the app under a public developer email in Play Console, that address is also a valid contact.
